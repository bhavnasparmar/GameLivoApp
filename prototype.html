Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect your information when you use our multi-game platform ("the App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

Account information: name, email address and profile avatar when you register.

Gameplay data: match results, scores, in-app currency and achievements.

Device & diagnostic data: device model, OS version, crash logs and app performance data.

Optional data: contacts (only if you enable "Find friends") and microphone access (only if you enable voice chat).

3. How we use your information

  • To operate matchmaking, leaderboards and rewards
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud
  • To improve app stability through crash & performance diagnostics
  • To send you match invites and product updates (you can opt out anytime)

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal data. Limited diagnostic data may be shared with our analytics and crash-reporting providers solely to improve the App. We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Data retention & deletion

You can request a copy of your data or delete your account at any time from Settings → Privacy & Security. Deleted account data is permanently removed from our active systems within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information, though no method of transmission is 100% secure.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be notified in-app before they take effect.

9. Contact us

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@ourgameapp.com.
